Senior Engineer, Video Analytics at Blaize

I build real-time computer vision systems on custom AI accelerators — the customer-facing Python API and the kernels underneath it doing post-processing on the chip.

The layer between the chip and the problem.

Blaize builds an AI accelerator. I build the software that makes it useful to somebody who has a camera and a problem. In practice that is an analytics platform — components, models, types, rendering, and deployment.

The other half is performance. An edge box has a fixed compute budget, and the usual way to lose it is shipping frames back to the host for work the accelerator could have done. Most of my optimisation work is closing that gap.
